Character sprites, dungeon tiles, item images, mouse cursors, sound effects and music in this game are derived from Flare: Empyrean Campaign art.

Source: https://github.com/flareteam/flare-game (commit af6eee6d339ac98011864bfe89da837fe7769c28)
License: CC-BY-SA 3.0 - https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
The derived sprite sheets are also licensed under CC-BY-SA 3.0.
Modifications: selected frames, equipment layers (weapon, armor, helm, boots; legs and hands merged) scaled by 0.32,
necromancer layers recolored,
alpha thresholded, reduced to 256 colors. Dungeon tiles: floor and wall faces flattened
from isometric into square textures, wall brightened; props and stairs scaled by 0.221.
Sound effects and music: copied unchanged (Ogg Vorbis). freeze.ogg: mods/fantasycore/soundfx/powers/freeze.ogg.
Items: drop animations scaled by 0.32 (potion by 0.5), item icons cropped from the icon atlas.

Demon (ruins boss): "Demon - Flare sprite sheets", OpenGameArt, CC0 - https://opengameart.org/content/demon-flare-sprite-sheets
Modifications: selected frames, saturation and brightness lowered, scaled by 0.64, alpha thresholded, reduced to 256 colors.

Time witch (ending): female avatar mage hood, vest, skirt, sleeves and boots recolored black and purple; kneeling frame made by cutting the knees out of the idle frame.

Fallen paladin (boss): "8 Directional Greatsword Knight" by Hormelz, itch.io, CC0 - https://hormelz.itch.io/8-directional-great-sword-knight-character
Modifications: idle frames halved, recolored (32-color palette swap), hit and death frames made by rotating the idle frame, scaled by 0.5, alpha thresholded, reduced to 256 colors.

Lake planks, trees, plants and log walls (act 2 lake): "Regular Grassland Tileset Hills / Slopes / Ramps [16x190]" by WithinAmnesia (an edit of the Flare
grassland tileset by Clint Bellanger), OpenGameArt, CC-BY-SA 3.0 - https://opengameart.org/content/regular-grassland-tileset-hills-slopes-ramps-16x190
Modifications: plank tiles 345, 347 flattened from isometric into square textures; 16 trees and 16 plants cut out (shadows and
neighbouring pieces removed), scaled by 42.43/64 and packed into lake_trees / lake_plants; a log-cabin wall face cropped into
wall_log.png (tools/lake.py, source kept in tools/art/grassland_plus).

Expedition tents (act 2 camp): "isometric props and tents" by rubberduck, OpenGameArt, CC0 - https://opengameart.org/content/isometric-props-and-tents
Modifications: six tents cropped from props_2x2.png / props_4x4.png (kept in tools/art/rubberduck), scaled by 42.43/128,
alpha thresholded, packed into one sheet (tools/tents.py), reduced to 256 colors.

Time warden (act 2 boss): original 3D model built from primitives by script (tools/boss3d/time_warden.js) and rendered
into an 8-direction sprite sheet (tools/boss3d/render.py); procedural canvas textures. Design reference: the time guardian
of the previous game (dungeon-crawler images/monsters/timeguardian.png, same author).

Runes (rune stones): "FLARE Item Variation 60x60 ONLY", OpenGameArt, CC0 - https://opengameart.org/content/flare-item-variation-60x60-only
Modifications: 20 rune stones cropped from the 5x4 preview sheet (tools/art/Runes1.png), black background made transparent,
packed into a one-column 40x40 sheet (tools/runes.py). One stone (row 4, column 2) is not used.
